Simple. Clean. Precise.

Metronome was created to give musicians an uninterrupted, ad-free practice and stage experience. Open the app, set your tempo, and play — with zero ads, no in-app purchases, and no pop-ups.

TEMPO & PRECISION
• Wide tempo range from 30 to 300 BPM with quick slider and fine +/- 1 BPM controls
• Dynamic Italian tempo markings (from Largo to Prestissimo)
• Accurate Tap Tempo to lock in any groove on the fly
• Clear visual beat indicator that stays in sync with every pulse

TIME SIGNATURES & SUBDIVISIONS
• Standard meters (2/4, 3/4, 4/4, 6/8) plus custom time signatures (1–16 beats per bar, denominators 2, 4, 8, and 16)
• Toggleable Beat 1 accent and natural compound-meter accents
• Four subdivisions: Quarter, Eighths, Triplets, and Sixteenths

SOUNDS & MIXER
• Four distinct timbres: Click, Wood Block, Beep, and Drums
• Independent volume sliders for Strong Beat, Weak Beats, and Subdivisions
• Visual-only mute mode to silence audio while keeping the visual pulse active

PRESETS & SETLISTS
• Save tempos, meters, subdivisions, and rehearsal notes as reusable presets
• Search, favorite, duplicate, and organize presets in one tap
• Build ordered setlists for gigs and rehearsals with drag-to-reorder and bar-synced transitions ("Next Bar")

BUILT FOR REAL PRACTICE & STAGE USE
• Background playback with media notification and lock screen controls
• Always-accessible Play/Stop action from any screen
• Light, Dark, and System themes for phones and tablets
• Available in English and Portuguese

NO ADS. NO DISTRACTIONS.
Metronome is 100% free, ad-free, and open source — built only with what you need to master your rhythm.
